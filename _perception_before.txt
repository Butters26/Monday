  - perception envelope fed to AttentionLobe competing signals
  - novelty_signal to novelty lobe when novel entities/concepts
  - conversation folds perception entities/concepts into understanding

  - SensoryIntegrationLobe — NOT in create_core_systems (orphaned;
    only used in integration_test.py)

    into Perception intake path

  - audio/vision: honest probes — disabled when device/libs missing;
    NO file-based processing path exists (so hearing/vision fail closed
    on headless boxes even when WAV/PNG files are available)
