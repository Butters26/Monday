MODULE
  autonomous_speech.py → AutonomousSpeechSystem
  Lobe name on live path: "speech" (already present in Thalamus learning rules).

ONE-LINER
  Social WHEN/WHETHER filter for already-formed speak-worthy autonomous
  thoughts — decides if an aside may leave the mouth this turn; does not
  invent wording and does not synthesize audio.

DONE_MEANS
  "speech" is registered on the live prompted path. Before Thalamus attaches
  a speak-worthy aside to the reply, it consults speech.evaluate_thought.
  If should_speak is False, the aside is requeued (temporary social block) or
  demoted (content inappropriate) — never attached. On user turns, speech
  is notified (user_spoke / conversation_active). On successful aside attach,
  speech is notified (speech_delivered). Voice still synthesizes the final
  Output text; Language still owns wording; AutonomousThinkingLoop still
  owns thought generation + speak_worthy minting.

OWNERSHIP BOUNDARIES
  AutonomousSpeech ("speech") owns:
    - Social timing / interruption awareness for unsolicited speak-worthy
      thoughts (typing/busy/interval/conversation-active heuristics)
    - Content appropriateness filter for speaking (e.g. too robotic → keep
      internal)
    - SpeechDecision envelope {should_speak, reason, timing, priority}
  Voice owns (DO NOT STEAL / DO NOT BREAK):
    - Speech synthesis / voice profile / audio envelope from text
    - Honest synthesized | play_unavailable | played | no_tts_backend
    - Fresh-user isolation of last envelopes
  Language owns: wording / sentence construction (speech does NOT compose
    reply text on the live path)
  Output owns: delivery / final reply envelope
  AutonomousThinkingLoop owns: generating autonomous thoughts + speak_worthy
    minting / satiation / relevance gate (speech does not invent thoughts)
  MetaAwareness owns: process / dual-stream notice of asides (observe only;
    does not veto attach — speech is the social veto, MetaAwareness remains
    notice-only)
  MetaCognition owns: epistemic watch (untouched)
  Social owns: continuity / stance cues (speech may consume conversation
    activity signals; does not reclassify social cues)
  Thalamus: tiny glue only — evaluate before aside attach; notify user_spoke /
    speech_delivered; stash last_speech_decision for diagnostics
  Motor / Pattern / Emotion / Reasoning / Executive / Attention: untouched
    except Executive's existing speak_worthy_aside inhibit (already in place)
  Learning stack: DO NOT TOUCH
  dual_stream_thinking.py: DO NOT WIRE
