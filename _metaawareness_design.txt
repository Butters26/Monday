  MetaAwareness owns:
    - Awareness of thinking PROCESS (mode: wandering | focused | transition)
    - Dual-stream tracking: spontaneous (wandering) vs controlled (focused)
    - Notice / engage / dismiss decisions for spontaneous thoughts
    - Process-awareness envelope (mode, streams, engagement, meta_comment)
  MetaCognition owns (DO NOT STEAL / DO NOT BREAK):
    - Epistemic watching of reasoning/language outputs
    - Empty grounding, uncertainty, overconfidence, surface contradiction
    - Control signals: force_grounded_refusal / flag_uncertainty / nudge_attention
  Boundary one-liner:
    MetaCognition = "Is this thought epistemically OK?"
    MetaAwareness = "Which stream am I in, and is this spontaneous thought
                     worth engaging?"
  Conversation=intent; Reasoning=grounded meaning; Language=wording;
  Output=delivery; Executive=goals; Pattern=relationships; Social=continuity;
  Motor=plan/queue; Voice=speech; Autonomous=inner thought generation.
  MetaAwareness does not classify intent, invent facts, compose replies,
  plan actions, synthesize speech, or replace MetaCognition.
