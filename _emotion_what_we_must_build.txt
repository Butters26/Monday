Monday emotion — what we must actually build
=============================================
Date: 2026-09-25 | HEAD baseline: a2925d9 | Research + diagnosis, no code.

Goal Matthew stated: emotion like his (human-shaped), working for real, not lied to.
Honest limit: this gets a believable continuous affect model. It does not make her biologically human.

Science we are using (not cargo-cult):
- Russell core affect: continuous valence + arousal is the substrate; emotion names are readout.
- OCC/appraisal: emotions come from evaluating events vs the agent's goals/standards — NOT phrase lists.
- Affective systems separate user-emotion recognition from the agent's own emotion generation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE MUST BUILD (bill of materials)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

1) MondayCoreAffect (new small core inside emotion lobe)
   - State: valence, arousal (keep dominance only if something real uses it; else drop)
   - Dynamics: decay toward setpoint each turn/time; additive deltas; clamp [-1,1]
   - NO intensity hardcode to 0.8; NO RNG among top labels as identity
   - Persist in emotional_state.json (already exists) — load/save this only first

2) SelfImpact (replaces phrase→interpersonal-event as the DRIVER of HER mood)
   - Inputs that may move HER core affect:
     a) Things about her / aimed at her (praise, insult, abandonment of her, gift to her)
     b) Her goals/needs status when Executive/Notus provide them later
     c) Gentle internal thought deltas (capped) — not full user-utterance classifiers
   - Explicit feeling words ABOUT THE USER ("I'm sad") do NOT write her core affect
   - Output: delta(v,a) + reason string for debug — not a fake "rejection" ontology for sadness

3) Label readout (thin)
   - Map (v,a) → one of a SMALL honest set: e.g. calm, happy, sad, angry, worried, scared, excited
   - Blends later only if proven needed; delete unused fancy enum members from live path
   - Label follows state; state does not follow a keyword table

4) Kill / rip from live path (not optional polish)
   - AppraisalEngine first-match _CLASSIFIERS as primary driver
   - "I'm sad" → rejection (and soft-event / semantic prototype copies of that map)
   - _switch_to_emotion intensity lottery / hardcoded 0.8
   - Thalamus/emotion treating user utterance event as her mood
   - appraise_internal_event re-running user substring tables at full strength

5) User-feeling channel (OUTSIDE emotion engine)
   - Keep/fix in Conversation + Social (emotional_share / last_cue) OR one thin UserAffect reader
   - Emotion lobe MUST NOT own or require this to generate her mood
   - Empathy in Language reads user channel + her core affect separately

6) Thalamus contract
   - emotion envelope: monday_emotion, valence, arousal, intensity, tone (from HER state only)
   - user_affect: from Conversation/Social only; never merged into monday_emotion by default

7) Proof harness (required before any PASS)
   Scripted multi-turn:
   - "I'm sad" → her core affect does not jump to rejection/sad-as-her; user channel shows sad
   - Insult aimed at her → her valence drops and stays drifted next turn
   - Quiet turns → decay toward calm, no random flip
   - Internal thought → small nudge at most, no full phrase slam
