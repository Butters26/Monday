DONE_MEANS
  SocialContextLobe is registered on the live prompted path. It tracks
  per-user social context across turns (cues, relationship stance,
  greeting continuity) from Conversation-owned intent + surface social
  markers, and distributes a compact social_context envelope into the
  shared cycle so another lobe (Language) honestly uses it. It does not
  steal Conversation intent classification, Language sentence construction,
  Reasoning fact answers, or Executive goal control.

OWNERSHIP BOUNDARIES
  Social owns:
    - Per-user social state across turns (stance, last/recent cues,
      already_greeted, greeting_count, continuity label).
    - Cue extraction from Conversation understanding + mild social text
      markers (greeting / goodbye / check_in / emotional_share / other).
    - Emitting a compact social_context dict for Thalamus to feed forward.
  Conversation owns: intent / slots / ask_kind (Social consumes, does not re-classify).
  Language owns: sentence construction (Social supplies stance/continuity only).
  Reasoning owns: facts / conclusions (may see social_context; does not own it).
  Executive owns: goal/inhibition (already maps greeting→social goal; untouched).

SMALLEST ARCHITECTURAL CORRECTION
  1. Flesh social_context_lobe.py: observe_turn / get_context / process_message.
  2. Register in run_abin.create_core_systems (+ shutdown list).
  3. Tiny Thalamus glue after Conversation.understand:
       observe_turn → attach social_context onto reasoning.think input
       and onto semantic_input before Language.
  4. Tiny Language glue: _compose_greeting consumes social_context
       continuity/already_greeted (returning vs first-meet pools).
  No from-scratch rewrite; no Pattern/Attention/closed-lobe redesign.

LIVE SCENARIO (honest, from code)
  Multi-turn greeting continuity: first "hello" then second "hello".
  BEFORE: Social absent; Language may emit first-meet lines ("Nice to meet you")
          with no social_context on the think/language path.
  AFTER:  Social registered; second hello shows stance/continuity tracked
          and Language avoids first-meet phrasing when already_greeted.

HONEST LIMITS (expected)
  Surface heuristics only (intent + mild social markers). No deep theory-of-mind,
  no invented relationship lore, no Notus-backed social graph. Per-process
  in-memory state (not durable across restarts).
